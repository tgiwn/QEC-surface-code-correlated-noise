## Intended arXiv submission license

For the initial arXiv submission of the manuscript, the intended selection is
the **arXiv.org perpetual, non-exclusive license 1.0**.

This is the intended submission choice; it does not mean that an arXiv
submission has already been completed. The selection grants arXiv rights to
distribute the submitted work. It does not place the manuscript, figures, or
research data under Apache-2.0 merely because the project code uses that
license, and it is not a general CC BY-style public reuse grant.

Before final submission, the submitter must confirm that they have the right
to grant the license on behalf of all authors and check any applicable journal,
funder, and institutional requirements. The license selected for a posted
version is irrevocable.
